Help

Page-by-page guidance for using NestVector by CoreNucApps.

Use this guide to understand the main app areas and what each view is designed to do.

Stocks

Use Stocks to maintain a personal watchlist. Start typing a ticker symbol to see suggestions, select a match, then choose Add.

The page shows current prices, daily changes, NYSE and NASDAQ summaries, and top market movers. Select a watchlist row to open a details popup with status and history. Use Refresh to reload live data.

Back to top ↑

Trends

Use Trends to graph up to four market symbols. Change the time range, add a graph by typing a ticker, or remove an existing graph chip.

Ticker suggestions appear as you type. Graph values are normalized to percent change so different symbols can be compared more easily.

Back to top ↑

TSP

Use TSP to review Thrift Savings Plan fund prices. Pick a timeline to change the history window.

Each fund row shows the latest share price, recent status, update date, and a compact history graph. Select a fund row to open a details popup with a larger graph and fund information.

Back to top ↑

Retirement

Use Retirement to estimate gross FERS or CSRS annuity scenarios. Enter a title, choose the retirement system, and enter service years, high-three salary values, retirement age, and unused sick leave hours.

The Federal Retirement System section can be shown or hidden with its Include switch. Add optional retirement income rows such as Social Security, 401k income, military income, or law enforcement income. Only rows marked Include are added to Total Retirement Income.

Save scenarios locally, load or delete saved calculations, and open Report to compare saved scenarios.

Back to top ↑

Investments

Use Investments to save details for life insurance policies, CDs, bank accounts, Roth IRAs, brokerage accounts, annuities, and other investment holdings.

Enter account name, financial institution, account or policy number, investment type, amount, rate of return, tax treatment, beneficiary, maturity date, and notes. Select a saved row to load it for editing, use Delete to remove it, select column checkboxes to show or hide table fields, and choose a column header to sort displayed investments.

Back to top ↑

Preferences

Use Preferences to choose the app theme, currency display, and button style.

System follows the device setting, while Light and Dark force the app into that mode. Currency controls how money fields and financial totals are formatted. Button style can show icons and text, icons only, or text only. The selected preferences are saved on the device.

Important Notes

NestVector is informational only. Market data, TSP values, and retirement estimates should be independently verified before making financial, investment, tax, employment, or retirement decisions.

Market and TSP data may be delayed, incomplete, or unavailable depending on upstream data sources and network access.
