Explore the main areas in NestVector by CoreNucApps, from watchlists and market trends to retirement scenarios and saved investment records.

Main App Areas

Stocks

Watchlist management, quote display, NYSE and NASDAQ market panels, and top market movers.

Trends

Four-slot chart dashboard with ticker suggestions, removable graph chips, and shared time-range selection.

TSP

Thrift Savings Plan fund rows with latest prices, status, update dates, sparklines, and detail popups.

Retirement

FERS and CSRS gross annuity estimates with saved scenarios, additional income rows, and comparison reports.

Investments

Local investment records with account details, visible-column choices, sorting, and saved table rows.

Preferences

Theme, currency display, and button presentation controls saved locally on the device.
