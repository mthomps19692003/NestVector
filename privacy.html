Privacy Policy

NestVector by CoreNucApps

Effective date: May 29, 2026

Overview

NestVector by CoreNucApps is designed as an informational financial dashboard and local planning organizer. The app helps users track market symbols, review TSP fund information, estimate retirement scenarios, and save investment account records.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be stored, how it is used, when network requests are made, and what choices users have. This policy applies to the NestVector application and this supporting website.

Information Stored By The App

The application may store user-selected information locally on the device where the app is installed. This may include:

  • Watchlist ticker symbols and selected trend graph symbols.
  • Theme, currency display, button display mode, chart range, and other local preferences.
  • Cached TSP fund data from the most recent successful data request.
  • Saved retirement scenario titles, high-three salary values, service years, retirement age, sick leave hours, selected retirement system, and optional income rows.
  • Saved investment records such as account names, financial institutions, account or policy numbers, investment type, amount, rate of return, tax treatment, beneficiary, maturity date, and notes.

The app does not require users to create a CoreNucApps account to use these local features.

How Information Is Used

Information stored by the application is used to provide app features, including:

  • Displaying selected watchlist and trend symbols.
  • Remembering local display and formatting preferences.
  • Saving and comparing retirement scenarios.
  • Saving, editing, sorting, and displaying investment account records.
  • Showing the most recent available TSP information when current data cannot be loaded.

CoreNucApps does not use locally stored app data for advertising, profiling, or sale to third parties.

Network Requests And Data Sources

The app makes network requests to retrieve market and fund information requested by the user. These requests may include ticker symbols, search text, and date or range parameters needed to return quote and history data.

  • Yahoo Finance endpoints may be used for quotes, ticker suggestions, historical trends, NYSE and NASDAQ data, market movers, gold futures, and silver futures.
  • The official TSP fund price history CSV may be used for Thrift Savings Plan fund share-price history.

External data providers may receive normal technical information such as IP address, user agent, requested URL, request time, and related network metadata according to their own practices.

Information We Do Not Collect

CoreNucApps does not intentionally collect the following through the app:

  • Payment card numbers or bank account login credentials.
  • Brokerage account login credentials or trade instructions.
  • Government identification numbers.
  • Precise device location.
  • Contacts, photos, camera content, or microphone recordings.
  • Advertising identifiers for targeted advertising.

Users should avoid entering sensitive personal information into notes or account fields unless they are comfortable storing that information locally in the app.

Local Storage, Retention, And Deletion

App data remains on the device until the user edits it, deletes it, uninstalls the app, clears app data, or otherwise removes the underlying files from the device.

Users can manage saved retirement scenarios and investment records from within the app where supported. Device-level backups, cloud sync tools, antivirus tools, or operating-system services controlled by the user may also process files stored on the device.

Website Privacy

This supporting website is a static informational website. It provides product information, help content, screenshots, technology details, release notes, and this Privacy Policy.

The website does not include account sign-in, payment processing, comments, or forms that intentionally collect personal information. If the site is hosted by a third-party platform, that platform may process standard technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, request logs, and security telemetry according to its own policies.

Financial Disclaimer

NestVector is informational only. Market data, TSP values, and retirement estimates may be delayed, incomplete, inaccurate, or unavailable. Users should independently verify information before making financial, investment, tax, employment, or retirement decisions.

Changes To This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect changes to the app, website, data sources, or support practices. When this page is updated, the effective date may also be updated.
