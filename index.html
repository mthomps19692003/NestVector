About the App

NestVector by CoreNucApps is built for users who want a practical way to follow markets and keep personal planning details close at hand. The app combines stock and fund watchlists, market movers, trend charts, TSP fund history, retirement calculations, and locally saved investment records in one application.

It is intended as an informational dashboard and planning organizer. It does not provide financial advice, trade execution, tax reporting, or brokerage account management.

Key Features

Stock Watchlist

Add and remove ticker symbols, view current quote data, open detail popups, and refresh market information when needed.

Market Overview

Review NYSE and NASDAQ index summaries, top market movers, and focused market status panels.

Trend Charts

Track up to four selected symbols and compare normalized movement across timelines from one day through five years.

TSP Funds

Review Thrift Savings Plan fund prices, daily status, compact history graphs, and fund detail views.

Retirement Scenarios

Estimate gross FERS or CSRS annuity income, add optional income sources, save scenarios locally, and compare reports.

Investment Records

Track life insurance policies, CDs, bank accounts, Roth IRAs, annuities, brokerage accounts, and other holdings.

Who It Is For

  • Individual investors maintaining a personal stock or fund watchlist.
  • Federal employees or retirees reviewing FERS, CSRS, and TSP information.
  • People organizing investment account details for planning conversations.
  • Users who want a local-first app for saved retirement and investment scenarios.
